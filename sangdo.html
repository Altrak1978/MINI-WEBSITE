Hydraulic Breakers

Sangdo hydraulic breakers are designed for demanding construction and demolition work. The range includes standard open-type and silenced box-housing breakers, supported by different tool sizes and carrier capacities for a variety of jobsite applications.

Product Range & Applications

Explore the Sangdo SD Series, including open-type and silenced-type hydraulic breakers, valve technology, and attachments for different equipment applications.

SD Series

Standard Type (Open Type)

The open-type design supports easier access for maintenance and servicing. The brochure lists models from SD50 through SD800, covering a range of breaker sizes and applicable carrier classes.

SD Series

Silenced Type (Box Housing)

The box-housing design helps protect the breaker body and reduce noise. It is presented in the brochure for applications where noise control is an important operating consideration.

Technology

Valve Type

The brochure illustrates I-valve built-out and I-valve built-in configurations, showing the main internal components of the hydraulic breaker assembly.

Attachments & Applications

Demolition Attachments

The product range also features attachments such as bucket crusher, vibro hammer, vibro ripper, grapples, compactor, crusher, and other equipment shown in the brochure.
